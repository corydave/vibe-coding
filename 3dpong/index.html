How to play
  • Keep the yellow ball from getting past your cyan paddle (the one closest to you).
  • Mouse: point where you want your paddle.
  • Touch: drag your finger on the screen.
  • Keyboard: Arrow keys or W A S D.
  • Hit the ball with the edge of your paddle to angle it. Moving the paddle as you hit adds spin.
  • P, Space or Esc pauses. C changes camera motion. M mutes sound.
  • The yellow ring around the box shows how far away the ball is. The longer the rally, the faster everything spins.
Put it on your home screen

Heads up: the camera spins constantly and the screen flashes when points are scored. If motion or flashing bothers you, set Camera motion to "Off".
