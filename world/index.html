Campsite at Dusk

Click inside the scene, then walk with the arrow keys or W A S D. Drag with the mouse to look around. Click the campfire to light it.

What is in this world

You are standing in a grassy clearing at dusk, under a warm orange sky. A few steps in front of you is an unlit campfire: two crossed logs with a small orange flame. A gray rock sits just to its right, for sitting on. Beyond the fire, slightly to the left, is a yellow tent shaped like a pyramid. Three pine trees stand around the edge of the clearing, and off to the right, further away, is a round blue lake.

When the campfire is lit, the flame grows tall and bright and casts an orange glow on everything nearby.
