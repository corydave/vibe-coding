Peanut Butter Oatmeal Cookies

Makes about 18. Twenty minutes, start to finish.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • A pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Stir the peanut butter, sugar, egg and vanilla together until smooth.
  3. Fold in the oats, baking soda and salt.
  4. Roll into walnut-sized balls and flatten slightly on a lined tray.
  5. Bake 10 to 12 minutes. They will look underdone. Take them out anyway.
  6. Let them sit on the tray for five minutes before moving them.
