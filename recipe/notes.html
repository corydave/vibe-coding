Notes

  • Natural peanut butter separates and makes the dough greasy. The cheap stuff works better here, which feels wrong but is true.
  • Ten minutes, not twelve. They keep cooking on the hot tray.
  • Doubling the batch does not double the baking time. Learned the hard way.
  • Quick oats make them cakey. Rolled oats make them chewy.
